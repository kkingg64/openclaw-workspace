## 🎯 商業機會建議

### 機會 1: AI Workflow Template Marketplace
**商機:** 製作同銷售預設既 AI Workflow Templates
- **平台:** Gumroad, Etsy, 自建 Shopify
- **定價:** $29-$199 per template
- **熱門模板:**
  - AI Lead Qualification Flow
  - Automated Content Repurposing
  - AI Customer Support triage
  - Invoice Processing Automation
- **成本:** 邊際成本接近零，純利 90%+

### 機會 2: No-Code AI Agency
**商機:** 幫中小企建立 AI Workflow
- **服務內容:**
  - 流程咨詢 + 設計
  - Make.com/Zapier 搭建
  - 培訓 + 維護
- **定價:**
  - Setup: $500-$2,000
  - Monthly retainers: $200-$1,000/mo
- **目標客戶:** Local businesses, E-commerce, Agencies

### 機會 3: AI Workflow Course Creator
**商機:** 教人點樣用 AI Workflow 工具
- **平台:** Udemy, Skillshare, 自營銷 (Teachable)
- **內容:**
  - Make.com 完整教學
  - Zapier AI Mastery
  - AI Agent Building for Beginners
- **定價:** $49-$297 per course
- **優勢:** Passive income，一次製作長期收益

### 機會 4: Vertical AI Agent (E-commerce)
**商機:** 為電商提供專用 AI Agent
- **功能:**
  - Product description generator
  - Review response automation
  - Inventory alert system
  - Customer segmentation
- **定價:** $99-$499/month
- **目標客戶:** Shopify, WooCommerce 商家
