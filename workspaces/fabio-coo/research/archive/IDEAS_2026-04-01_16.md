### 3. The "3-Second Hook" Algorithm Warfare — New Rules for Short Video Retention

**Insight:** TikTok and Reels have evolved past "hook" — now it's **"pattern interrupt"** in the first 1.5 seconds that determines whether the algorithm pushes your content.

**What's actually working (retention-wise):**
- **Start mid-action:** Don't show the beginning — start at the most interesting moment
- **Text overlay that lies/misleads:** "Wait, this isn't what you think" → curiosity gap
- **Rapid context switching:** First frame must look completely different from frame 5
- **No smiling in the first second:** Counterintuitive — intense expressions beat warm smiles for hook
- **Vertical bar charts / progress indicators:** "73% of people don't know this" → immediate curiosity

**The data:**
- Videos with hooks in first 1.5 seconds: 40-60% avg watch time
- Videos with hooks at 3+ seconds: 15-25% avg watch time
- Algorithm now weights "replay rate" over "likes" — if people re-watch, it multiplies distribution

**Commercial Value:**
- **Short video hook writing service:** Write + test 10 hook variants for a creator's content → charge per batch
- **Hook A/B testing tool:** Platform that automatically tests multiple hook styles and reports which wins
- **Retention analysis service:** Review client's existing Shorts → identify where viewers are dropping → rescript and reshoot
- **Hook swipe file database:** Curated library of viral hooks organized by niche and format type (subscription model)

**Action:** Watch 50 viral Shorts from the past 30 days — catalog the first 1.5 seconds of each → build your personal "hook pattern library"

## 🎵 Trending Sound & Format Patterns

**Sounds driving viral in Q1 2026:**
- **AI-generated original music:** Creators using AI music generators (Suno, Udio) for unique background tracks — no licensing issues, algorithm favors fresh audio
- **"Speed-run" narration:** 2-3x speed voice-over over fast-cut visuals
- **White noise + talking head:** Minimalist "study with me" style but for opinions/analysis
- **Remixed viral songs:** Taking a 6-month-old viral sound and applying it to a new context = freshness + proven viral potential

**Format winners:**
- **"Listicle in motion":** "5 things I wish I knew before [X]" — fast cuts between each point
- **Screen record + reaction:** Mobile screen recordings with real-time emotional reactions
- **"Comment section storytime":** Reading funny/dramatic comments as the script for the video
