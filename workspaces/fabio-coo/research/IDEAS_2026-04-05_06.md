```
HIGH-PERFORMING SHORT VIDEO FORMATS
────────────────────────────────────
1. POV (Point of View) Storytelling
   - "As a [profession], here's what I see..."
   - Authentic perspective, not staged
   
2. Before/After Transformations
   - Makeover, progress, results
   - Works for: fitness, beauty, home, business
   
3. "What I Actually Use" Recommendations
   - Genuine reviews, not ads
   - Tools, products, services
   
4. Tutorial + Demo Hybrids
   - "How to [action] in 60 seconds"
   - Educational + actionable
   
5. Day-in-the-Life (DiRL)
   - Authentic routine content
   - Builds parasocial connection
```
