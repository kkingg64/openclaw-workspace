## 💡 商機識別

### 🎯 機會 1: 垂直領域 AI Agent Marketplace (B2B SaaS)

**痛點:**
- 企業想要 Industry-Specific 既 AI Agents，但市場上既general-purpose agents 唔夠專業
- 每個行业都有獨特既 regulatory requirements、workflows、terminology
- Build 自有 AI Agent 既成本高，企業寧願購買現成既

**解決方案:**
- 聚集垂直領域既 AI Agents (Legal, Healthcare, Finance, etc.)
- 提供認證機制，確保 quality 同 compliance
- 企業可以直接購買、部署、管理 AI Agents

**商業模式:**
- Marketplace 抽佣 15-30%
- Agent 認證收費 $500-5000/agent
- Enterprise subscriptions $999-9999/month

**市場規模:**
- Enterprise AI Agent Market: $150B (2026)
- Vertical AI Agents: $50B+

### 🎯 機會 5: AI Agent Orchestration Platform

**痛點:**
- 企業需要管理多個 AI Agents，但冇統一既platform
- Agents 之間既 communication、workflow、permissions 需要協調
- 缺乏 enterprise-grade 既 agent management

**解決方案:**
- Platform 連接多個 AI Agents
- 統一既 workflow orchestration
- Enterprise-grade security、logging、monitoring

**功能:**
1. **Multi-Agent Orchestration** - 協調多个 AI agents 协同工作
2. **Workflow Builder** - 無代碼 build agent workflows
3. **Agent Marketplace** - 內建 marketplace 購買第三方 agents
4. **Enterprise Controls** - SSO、audit logs、role-based access

**定價:**
- Starter: $299/month (5 agents)
- Pro: $999/month (25 agents)
- Enterprise: $5000+/month (無限 agents)
