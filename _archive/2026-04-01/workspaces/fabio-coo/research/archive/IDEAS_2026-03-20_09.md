- **應用場景**: Product demos, Tutorial, Storytelling

- **TikTok Search**: 超越 Google among Gen-Z
- **關鍵字**: #TikTokMadeMeBuyIt, #ShopLocal, #Sustainable
- **長尾效應**: 內容可以持續獲得流量 3-6 months
