### 💡 變現機會分析

#### High Potential Opportunities:

**1. AI Clean Room Service**
- **痛點:** 企業需要安全既數據協作環境 (Malus 1095 points!)
- **解決方案:** Clean Room as a Service for AI training/inference
- **定價:** $999/mo (Pro), Custom Enterprise
- **市場:** 醫療、金融、广告、 Research
- **門檻:** 需要 data engineering 能力

**2. Agent Security Audit Platform**
- **痛點:** RAG poisoning、prompt injection 等攻擊增加
- **解決方案:** Automated security scanning for AI agents
- **定價:** $199/mo (Pro), Custom Enterprise
- **類似:** Snyk for AI
- **市場:** 所有 AI 開發公司

**3. AI Hedge Fund / Trading Agent**
- **痛點:** 金融市場數據處理需求
- **解決方案:** Pre-built trading agents with market data integration
- **定價:** Subscription + performance fee
- **驗證:** ai-hedge-fund (2.3k stars) 證明需求存在
- **風險:** 高，但回報高

**4. Edge Local Agent Stack**
- **痛點:** 數據隐私、本地部署需求
- **解決方案:** Self-hosted agent infrastructure (似 airi)
- **定價:** $49/mo (Pro), Enterprise custom
- **市場:** 企業内部、敏感數據行業

**5. Agent Performance Benchmark Platform**
- **痛點:** 難以客觀比較不同 agent 既表現
- **解決方案:** Standardized benchmarks with leaderboard
- **定價:** Free (社區) + $99/mo (Pro API)
- **類似:** HuggingFace Leaderboard for Agents
