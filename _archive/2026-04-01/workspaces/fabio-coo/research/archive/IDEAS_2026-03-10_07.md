## 🚀 垂直 AI Agents 商業機會

### 機會 1: Legal AI Agents

**缺口:** 律師事務所需要文件審閱、合約分析既 AI

**可行方向:**
- Contract review agent (找出風險條款)
- Due diligence automation
- Case law research assistant
- Legal document generator

**市場規模:**
- 全球 legal tech market: $25B (2026 估計)
- 中小律所 budget: $5K-50K/年

**參考產品:**
- Harvey AI (已融資 $100M+)
- Casetext (被 Thomson Reuters 收購)

**建議:** 做 SMB-friendly version，$99-499/月

---

### 機會 2: Finance AI Agents

**缺口:** 中小企業需要 financial analysis 但請唔起分析師

**可行方向:**
- Accounts payable/receivable automation
- Cash flow prediction agent
- Expense categorization + insights
- Tax preparation assistant

**市場規模:**
- SMB accounting software: $15B+
- 痛點: Xero/QuickBooks 功能多但唔夠智能

**建議:** Build on top of accounting APIs，做 AI layer

---

### 機會 3: Healthcare AI Agents

**缺口:** 醫療機構需要 appointment scheduling、patient triage

**可行方向:**
- Appointment booking agent
- Insurance claim processing
- Patient follow-up automation
- Medical transcription + summarization

**門檻:** 需要 HIPAA compliance，但利潤高

**參考:**
- Olive AI (已融資 $1.3B)
- Hippocratic AI ($141M Series A)

**建議:** 做簡單既 appointment + billing agent 先

---

### 機會 4: Real Estate AI Agents

**缺口:** 地產經紀需要 lead generation + client management

**可行方向:**
- Property listing generator
- Lead qualification agent
- Market analysis reports
- Automated follow-up sequences

**市場:**
- Global real estate tech: $15B+
- 地產經紀願意花錢买 leads
